Is there any brokerage or agent commission? ▾
No, there is zero brokerage. You deal directly with the PG owner and management. You do not have to pay any agent or broker charges.

Yes, healthy home-cooked breakfast, lunch, and dinner are included in the monthly rent. We serve clean Gujarati and North Indian meals with fresh rotis, sabzi, dal, and rice.

The security deposit is equal to 1 month rent. It is 100% refundable when you move out, with no hassle.
